## Humanizer writing-pattern guidance

Scout's `lib/scout/human-writing.ts` style guidance is an independent, condensed adaptation inspired by concepts from [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), version 2.11.2 at the time of integration. Humanizer is MIT-licensed and bases its pattern list on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" / WikiProject AI Cleanup guidance.

Scout does not vendor the Humanizer skill wholesale. The Scout-specific implementation keeps the project's stricter provenance rules: approved claim IDs remain pinned, new factual anchors are rejected, contact evidence cannot be invented, and humanization cannot enable email sending or application submission.
