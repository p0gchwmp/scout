# M13 — Capital & Startup Ecosystem Discovery

## Goal
Broaden Scout beyond ordinary job boards into capital, startup and ecosystem opportunity surfaces without turning Scout into a private-contact scraper, pitch-spam bot or autonomous applicant.

The curated registry is a bootstrap, **not an allowlist**. Registered official sources receive stronger routing. Search-index discoveries outside the registry may surface only as review-required leads and cannot become trusted contact/job evidence until their official institution or employer source is reviewed and resolved.

## Opportunity lanes
- Venture capital and seed funds
- VC/investor portfolio talent boards
- Accelerators and incubators
- Venture/startup studios
- Angel networks and syndicates
- Family offices
- Impact, climate and health investors
- Corporate venture capital and innovation teams
- Growth equity and selected early-career private equity
- Search funds / ETA
- Venture fellowships and scout programs
- Portfolio Founder’s Office, strategy and business-operations roles
- Startup/operator talent networks
- University incubators and student venture funds
- Health-innovation accelerators
- Think tanks and economic-research institutions
- Development-finance and multilateral innovation roles

## Core paths
1. Formal role on a registered official institution careers page -> preserve official evidence -> existing Scout ranking/review/application packet -> Safe Fill where supported -> Ryan manually submits.
2. Investor/accelerator portfolio-board role -> lead only -> resolve the portfolio company’s official employer/ATS posting -> existing Scout application pipeline.
3. Unregistered ecosystem result from search -> review-only lead -> verify/register official institution/employer -> then resolve official role before application preparation or contact research.
4. No formal opening at a registered recruiting target -> research explicitly published public recruiting/business contacts -> every new contact stays `needs_review` -> accepted contact can feed existing claim-backed outreach drafting -> Gmail draft only.

## Bootstrap source registry
Includes Blume, Chiratae, Peak XV, Surge, Nexus, Accel, a16z, General Catalyst, Lightspeed, Khosla Ventures, 8VC, Bain Capital Ventures, 500 Global, Techstars and Antler. This list is deliberately non-exhaustive.

## Contact rules
Allowed: explicitly published careers/recruiting/talent/HR/college-talent business emails and official business contacts with exact URL/excerpt provenance. Generic public addresses can be retained only for human review.

Rejected from recruiting use unless the same evidence explicitly says careers/recruiting: startup-pitch, deal-flow, media/press, LP/investor/fundraising, founder-program or demo-day application inboxes.

Never guess email patterns, scrape LinkedIn/private profiles, use personal-contact/data-broker pages, harvest personal phone/WhatsApp/address data, bypass CAPTCHA/rate limits/login, auto-send or auto-submit.
